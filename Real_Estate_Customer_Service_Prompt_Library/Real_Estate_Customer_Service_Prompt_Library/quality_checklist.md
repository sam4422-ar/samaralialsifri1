# Customer Reply — Quality Checklist

Review every generated reply before sending.

## Accuracy
- [yes] Does it answer the customer's actual question?
- [ yes] Are all prices, dates, statuses, policies, and process steps verified?
- [ yes] Does it avoid inventing a booking, escalation, approval, or resolution?
- [ yes] Are unknown facts clearly marked as needing confirmation?

## Customer experience
- [ yes] Is it in the appropriate language?
- [ yes] Is the tone professional and respectful?
- [ yes] If the customer is upset, does it acknowledge the concern without arguing?
- [yes ] Does it avoid blaming the customer or making unsupported promises?

## Clarity
- [ yes] Are the steps easy to understand?
- [yes ] If multiple questions were asked, are they answered in order?
- [yes ] Is the response suitable for the channel and length setting?
- [ yes] Does it end with a clear next step?

## Privacy and safety
- [yes ] Is unnecessary personal information omitted?
- [ yes] Are internal notes kept out of the customer-facing reply?
- [ yes] Does the case need review by an authorized employee?

## Approval
- [yes ] A human has reviewed the response.
- [ yes] The response is approved before sending.
