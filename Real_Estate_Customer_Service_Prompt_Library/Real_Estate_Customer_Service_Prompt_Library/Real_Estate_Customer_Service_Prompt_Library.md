# Real Estate Customer Service Prompt Library

## Project Overview
This project contains a reusable prompt library for customer service tasks at Dar AlSakan Realty, a fictional real estate office in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

## Purpose
The library helps customer service staff:
- Respond to customer inquiries and complaints.
- Classify incoming requests.
- Mask personal information.
- Identify issues that require escalation.
- Follow consistent privacy and safety rules.

## Repository Structure
- `blocks/`: Shared business facts.
- `templates/`: Five reusable prompt templates.
- `rules/`: Shared safety and privacy rules.
- `library_index.md`: Template index.
- `library_guide.md`: Instructions for using the library.
- `Test_Results_Report.pdf`: Test results and screenshots.

## Responsible AI Use
All outputs must be reviewed by a human before use when verification, sensitive information, payments, contracts, or legal matters are involved. The prompts must not invent facts, expose private information, make unsupported promises, or provide legal advice.

## Fictional Business Notice
Dar AlSakan Realty is a fictional business created for this educational project.
