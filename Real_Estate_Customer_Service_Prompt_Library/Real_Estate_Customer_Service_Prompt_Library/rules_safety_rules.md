# Shared Safety Rules

## Accuracy
- Do not invent prices, availability, policies, payment status, or resolution timelines.
- Clearly identify information that needs confirmation.
- Do not present assumptions as verified facts.

## Privacy
- Mask personal names, phone numbers, identification details, and personal email addresses before sharing customer messages when required.
- Do not reveal unmasked sensitive information in summaries.
- Preserve relevant business information while protecting personal data.

## Escalation
- Refer payment complaints, contract disputes, and legal matters to the relevant department.
- Do not claim that an issue has already been escalated unless this is verified.
- Do not provide legal advice or decide legal validity.

## Customer Communication
- Use professional, respectful, clear language.
- Do not promise a viewing or booking before availability is confirmed.
- Do not promise a refund or resolution before verification.

## Human Review
- Flag missing information and ambiguous cases.
- Recommend human review for sensitive, legal, payment, or unclear privacy issues.
- Review generated text before sending it to a customer.

## Limitations
The prompt library generates text only. It cannot independently verify transactions, confirm bookings, contact departments, approve refunds, or guarantee complete anonymization.
